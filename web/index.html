Annual values

Rent CPI measures rental price change, not advertised dollars. Earnings describe payroll employees and can change when the mix of jobs changes. Completions cover all areas, total dwelling units. These are different populations within the same province, so their growth does not establish causation or an individual's affordability. Monthly CPI/earnings are averaged only when all 12 months are published. Geographic definitions can change across census vintages. The current StatCan completion source has no numeric values after 2022; newer years remain visibly unavailable.
